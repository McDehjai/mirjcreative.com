Systems that run without you

Most marketing and ops teams lose hours every week to manual decisions and undocumented processes. Which channel carries this campaign? Who owns this handoff? When does it go live? These aren't just creative or people questions. They're operational ones, and they're costing your team time it doesn't have.

As part of the Operations & Automation Retainer, MIRJ Creative maps the decision logic behind how your team works (the "if this, then that" rules someone on your team is already making, just not written down anywhere), then builds the AI-powered systems that carry that logic forward. We fix the process gaps first, then automate what's left, refining both each month as your team and campaigns evolve.

The result is a team that executes faster, makes fewer reactive calls, and shows up to strategy conversations instead of spending the week in logistics.

Entry point

The Operations Audit or AI Automation Audit uncovers where manual decisions and process gaps are slowing you down, and quantifies the cost. That map becomes the foundation for everything built in the retainer.

What you build in the retainer

Operational systems, decision logic frameworks (documented rules for the calls your team makes on repeat), and AI workflow integrations, refined monthly as performance data comes in. You go in with a diagnosis and come out with a build plan.
