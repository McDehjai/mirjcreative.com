1. Services

MIRJ Creative LLC ("Consultant") agrees to provide the audit service selected above ("Services") to the undersigned client ("Client"). Scope, deliverables, and timing are as described in MIRJ Creative's published service descriptions and any intake materials the Client provides.

2. Deposit & Fees

Client agrees to pay a $375 deposit to begin the Services. This deposit is credited in full toward the final project total, which is typically $750 to $1,500 depending on scope and is confirmed in writing after intake and discovery. The $375 deposit is non-refundable once the working session is scheduled, per Section 5 below. The remaining balance is invoiced once scope is confirmed and is due in full before the delivery call described in Section 3.

3. Timeline

The engagement proceeds in four stages: agreement, intake and discovery, working session(s), and a delivery call held within 5 business days of the working session. The written deliverable is provided during the delivery call itself, not as a separate step. A 30-day follow-up window for questions is included at no additional charge.

4. Revisions

This engagement includes up to 2 rounds of revision on the delivered findings. Additional revisions beyond the included rounds will be billed at $250/hour or a flat fee agreed upon in writing, or, for a pro bono engagement, addressed as capacity allows and agreed upon between both parties.

5. Cancellation

Client may cancel with 7 days' written notice prior to the scheduled working session for a full refund of the deposit. Once the working session is scheduled and confirmed, the deposit becomes non-refundable. Work completed prior to cancellation will be billed at the agreed rate on a prorated basis. Once the final deliverable has been delivered at the delivery call, all services rendered are considered complete and no refunds will be issued.

6. Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of full payment where applicable, Client owns the final deliverables. MIRJ Creative retains ownership of all frameworks, tools, methodologies, and processes used to deliver the Services, and retains the right to reference the engagement in its portfolio in general terms unless Client requests otherwise in writing.

Client agrees not to reproduce, repurpose, distribute, or share MIRJ Creative's proprietary frameworks, tools, templates, intake materials, or methodologies with any third party, including other organizations, without MIRJ Creative's prior written consent. This restriction applies to MIRJ Creative's underlying materials, not to Client's own final deliverables, which Client is free to use internally as already described above.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep proprietary information, strategies, and communications shared during this engagement confidential, both during and after its term, except where disclosure is required by law or the information is already public.

8. Independent Contractor

MIRJ Creative is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client, and is solely responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and expenses.

9. Limitation of Liability

MIRJ Creative's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid, or, for a pro bono engagement where no fees are paid, shall not exceed the value of the deliverables provided. Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia. Disputes will first be addressed through good faith negotiation, then mediation in Virginia if unresolved, before any litigation.
