About MIRJ Creative

I help founders and small teams replace the manual grind of running a growing business with calm, working systems.

Great teams. Beautiful work. Broken processes. That's what 15+ years inside brand events, activations, and creative operations, at places like Capital One and MAC Cosmetics, taught me. The talent was never the issue. The systems were.

So the work grew. From planning and running the activation itself, to building, running, and redesigning the systems that keep a business afloat. Efficiently, and effectively.

Now I bring that same operational discipline to AI powered systems, helping founders and small business owners find what's actually broken and fix it for good.
